A warm editorial system in two riso inks — Fraunces sentence-case headings on warm newsprint, EB Garamond italic for the second voice, and a swappable Instrument component (fretboard, keyboard, etc) carrying the structural work.

The two presses + paper

Every Fretflow surface is "printed" with two ink plates. Anything beyond ink, spot, and paper is a mistake.

Ink

Ultramarine. Text, lines, default fills, the fretboard.

--ink-700 · #152066
Spot

Newsprint orange-red. Accent, misregistration echo, primary-CTA shadow.

--spot-700 · #FF5A2C
Paper

Warm newsprint. Never pure white — the page is a substrate, not a canvas.

--paper-100 · #F4EEDF

The instrument is the screen.

v4's central commitment. The structural visual on most product screens is a real instrument — not a sheet-music stave. The component is swappable; everything else stays put.

Adding a new instrument

Build a new component (Bass, Ukulele, Mandolin, etc.) that knows how to render itself and where the highlighted note(s) go. The Today / Practice / Review chrome is unchanged. Same paper, same ink, same Fraunces, same orange echo on the highlighted note.

Buttons

Primary CTA carries a hard 4×4 orange block shadow. Press collapses to zero — the button "settles onto the page". Ghost is the only secondary.
